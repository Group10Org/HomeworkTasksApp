3.Category: Productivity
Optimal stopping algorithm app
	-uses the Optimal stopping algorithm to pick the
	the best option out of a batch
    
The optimal stopping algorithm is a mathematical strategy for deciding when to stop searching and make a selection from a sequence of options to maximize the probability of picking the best one. A common example is the "37% rule," which suggests rejecting the first 37% of applicants (or options) and then choosing the next one that is better than all those seen so far. This strategy helps balance the need to gather information with the risk of missing the best option while waiting too long.
